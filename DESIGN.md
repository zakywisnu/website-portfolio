# Personal portfolio direction

Audience: people reviewing Ahmad Zaky Wisnumurti's iOS engineering work and writing.

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

The current green neutrals and résumé structure are preserved for this local animation experiment because they belong to the existing working design.

The hero uses a restrained field of Swift, UIKit, SwiftUI, Build, iOS, and Create words to introduce the owner's craft, inspired by the typographic animation on https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-wonder.

The owner's name, role, and contact links remain ordinary accessible HTML and the primary focal point; the decorative canvas is hidden from assistive technology.

A clear area around the name keeps the animation subordinate to the content. Motion updates slowly, pauses outside the viewport and in hidden tabs, and respects reduced motion. A keyboard-accessible button lets visitors pause it explicitly.

The preview adds no fabricated project imagery or claims. Existing uncommitted résumé changes are preserved.
